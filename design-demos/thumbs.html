Six skeletons — one per candidate, before any of them is a page

Same content in all six: the plate, the four layers, the filter grid, the missing green, the numbers at one site, the print. Only the skeleton changes. Three of these become full drafts.

1 · Bench with a fixed railControls never leave the left edge; the right side is a stage that swaps plates. Borrowed from: lab instruments, DAW mixers.

2 · Full-bleed stage, controls as a HUDThe frame is the whole viewport; the instrument floats over it. Borrowed from: camera viewfinders, flight displays.

3 · Contact sheetThe same frame printed at six stages, laid out in a grid, marked up like a proof. Borrowed from: darkroom contact sheets.

4 · One frame, marginaliaA single plate holds the page; type and numbers sit in the margins as annotations. Borrowed from: annotated photographic prints.

5 · The page is one splitMeasured on the left, guessed on the right, all the way down. The seam never closes. Borrowed from: before/after plates, dialectical spreads.

6 · Stepped splitThe split alternates sides every section, so the argument flips as you scroll. Borrowed from: magazine spreads that alternate columns.
